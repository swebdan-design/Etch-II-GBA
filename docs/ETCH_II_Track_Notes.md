# ETCH II — Track Notes

**ETCH II — A Generative Synthesis Machine** · by Synth Centric

Eight living compositions. Each begins in **DRIFT**: every four bars CORROSION rewrites a little of the bass and drums around the piece's own memory, and the sound drifts with it, so no two listens are the same and none wanders off. Each piece plays its song chain on a loop and can be left running.

**How they were made.** Every piece's first pattern is exactly what CORROSION generates from the piece's **legacy seed code** — enter that code on ETCH (the Game Boy original) and you hear the same groove through ETCH's chip voice. Further core patterns come from CORROSION's own operations (GENERATE with other settings, REGEN, fixed-seed CORRODE or DRIFT lineages), occasionally with a logged hand edit. The extension tracks — LEAD, CHORD, DRONE, TEXTURE — come from ETCH II's separate EXPANSION generator or are written by hand; they are **not** CORROSION output, and ETCH does not play them. The full recipe, pattern by pattern, is generated by `tools/compose_album.c` into `docs/generated_album_recipe.txt`.

**On ETCH (Game Boy):** the legacy code reproduces pattern 01's bass and four drum lanes. The rest of each arrangement (other patterns, extension tracks, sounds, chain) exists only on ETCH II.

## 1. OXIDE — industrial acid
`0A51-430B-6B90` · E phrygian · ACID HOUSE · 132 BPM

The opening statement: a driven, resonant saw bass in E phrygian, the flat second biting against the root, over a pounding analog kick and a bit-crushed DIGITAL snare. A low resonant phase-distortion drone and granular resonator ticks sit underneath; pattern 03 corrodes the bass six generations deep and adds a metallic FM lead with paraphonic SUB stabs; pattern 04 strips the kick for a breakdown. Master drive glues it. *Synthesis:* SUBTRACT (2× oversampled filter, high resonance, drive), FM 2-OP (modulator ratio 11), paraphonic SUB chord, PD RESO1 drone, RESONATR GRAIN.

## 2. FERRIC — metallic FM and percussion
`FE26-1049-7940` · C# minor · INDUSTRIAL · 126 BPM

Everything rings. The bass is two-operator FM with feedback — a struck iron bar more than a string — and the INDUSTRIAL grammar picks the CLICK kick, GRIT snare, SHARP and METAL hats, voiced as FM METAL so even the hats are frequency-modulated. A √2-ratio bell lead answers the bass; a wavetable METAL pad hums in the walls. *Synthesis:* FM 2-OP throughout, paraphonic WAVETABL chord, RESONATR MALLET texture.

## 3. VESSEL — dub techno
`7E55-5227-5630` · F dorian · MINIMAL · 118 BPM, swing 2

Space and delay. A round triangle bass, the MINIMAL grammar's hypnotic sparse kicks, and — written by hand — dub chord stabs on the off-beat (i – VII – iv – i, then i – iii – VII – v), voiced as paraphonic wavetable ORGAN chords and thrown into a long, dark ping-pong delay. A bowed resonator and a slow GLASS drone fill the room. Pattern 02 is eight generations of DRIFT away from the original. *Synthesis:* SUBTRACT triangle, paraphonic WAVETABL chords + delay, BOWED RESONATR, WAVETABL drone.

## 4. DEBRIS — broken electronic rhythms
`DEB5-743A-B961` · G minor pentatonic · BROKEN · 138 BPM, swing 4

Odd-length motifs falling across the bar line. A phase-distortion saw bass, a plucked resonator lead on a 12-step loop and a granular texture on a 7-step loop, so the three never line up the same way twice; DIGITAL drums with bit reduction; an ELECTRO interlude (pattern 04). The drone layer was removed so the busiest piece of the album stays inside the CPU budget. *Synthesis:* PD SAW bass, RESONATR PLUCK lead, paraphonic SUB square chords, RESONATR GRAIN, DIGITAL voicing.

## 5. STATIC — dark synthwave and digital melody
`5A71-201A-6751` · D minor · ELECTRO · 108 BPM

The album's song. A hand-written gliding melody in D minor (two phrases, A and B) on a DIGIT wavetable lead, held i – VI – III – VII pads, a pulse bass under ELECTRO drums (PUNCH kick), and phase-distortion shimmer. The drone layer was removed: the pads carry the harmony. *Synthesis:* SUBTRACT pulse bass, WAVETABL DIGIT lead with glide, paraphonic WAVETABL pads, PD RESO2 texture.

## 6. PATINA — atmospheric generative ambience
`9A71-A256-3361` · A# dorian · SPARSE · 86 BPM

The quietest piece. SPARSE grammar: long notes, ties and space. A soft HOLLOW wavetable bass, slow-attack VOICE pads, a bowed resonator texture on 7- and 12-step loops that drifts against the bar, and a low phase-distortion drone with a slow LFO; drums are present but distant, mostly in the delay. *Synthesis:* WAVETABL bass and pads, RESONATR BOWED, PD RESO2 drone, long ping-pong delay.

## 7. FRACTURE — complex rhythmic interplay
`F7AC-B33C-DA50` · B phrygian · BROKEN · 142 BPM, swing 2

Polymeter as the subject: the extension tracks run at 5, 7 and 12 steps against the 16-step core, so lead, chords and texture continually re-align. An FM bass with heavy feedback, a square lead, DIGIT wavetable stabs, FM drums. *Synthesis:* FM 2-OP bass and drone, SUBTRACT square lead, paraphonic WAVETABL chords, RESONATR MALLET texture, FM METAL voicing.

## 8. REMNANT — restrained, reflective
`2E77-0025-4441` · C minor · MINIMAL · 96 BPM

The coda. Low density, gentle mutation. A hand-written theme in C minor on a mallet resonator (A, then B), sustained i – VI – V and VII – iv – V organ chords, a soft triangle bass and a phase-distortion halo; the last pattern thins to density 3. (No drone: the organ chords hold the harmony.) *Synthesis:* SUBTRACT triangle, RESONATR MALLET lead, paraphonic WAVETABL ORGAN chords, PD RESO1 texture.

## Cohesion

The album moves from the hardest machine music (OXIDE, FERRIC) through space (VESSEL), fracture (DEBRIS), song (STATIC), atmosphere (PATINA), rhythmic complexity (FRACTURE) to reflection (REMNANT). All eight share CORROSION as the composer, the oxidised palette of the visual identity, and the same living behaviour: they begin in DRIFT and stay recognisably themselves while never repeating exactly.
